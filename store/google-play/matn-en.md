## Full description  (max 4000 chars)

```
e-Kassam is a point-of-sale and CRM system for shops in Uzbekistan. One app
serves both sides: the SHOP runs its trade, and the CUSTOMER sees their own
card, points and receipts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FOR CUSTOMERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• LOYALTY CARD — no plastic needed. Show the QR or barcode from your phone
  at the till. The code refreshes every 30 seconds, so a photo of it cannot
  be reused by someone else.

• YOUR POINTS — see how many points you have at each shop, when they
  expire and where they were spent.

• YOUR RECEIPTS — even if the paper slip is lost, the purchase history
  stays. Open any receipt to see what you bought.

• YOUR CREDIT — if the shop let you buy on credit, the balance and due date
  are on your phone. When the shop records a debt you are asked to CONFIRM
  it: you tap "confirm" or "reject". The "I never took that" dispute is now
  recorded on both sides.

• OFFERS — discounts announced by the shop.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FOR SHOPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• TILL — barcode scanner, weighed goods, split payment (cash + card), Click
  and Payme, several carts at once. The cashier can lower a price, and a
  receipt discount is distributed across the lines — so returns add up
  correctly.

• CREDIT — replaces the paper debt book. Due dates, reminders, customer
  confirmation and a payment receipt. Old debts can be entered by hand with
  their original date.

• STOCK — balances, intake, transfers, stocktaking. Goods close to their
  expiry date are highlighted, and stickers can be printed for them.

• WAREHOUSE PICKUP — the customer pays at the till, a storekeeper hands the
  goods over. They scan the code on the customer's receipt, see the list and
  tap "handed over". Who did it and when is recorded.

• OVERSIGHT — today's revenue, profit, expenses and stock losses on the home
  screen. Discounts, cancellations and cart removals stay in the audit log.

• STAFF — cashier, storekeeper, shop admin. Each has their own permissions;
  sensitive actions are confirmed with a manager badge.

• RECEIPTS AND HARDWARE — receipt printer, cash drawer, scales. Without a
  printer the receipt is printed from the browser.

• OFFLINE — if the connection drops the till DOES NOT STOP: the sale is
  queued and sent to the server once the connection is back.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Available in Uzbek, Russian and English.

To use it as a shop you need to register at e-kassam.uz. To sign in as a
customer, your phone number is enough.

Questions and feedback: __EMAIL__
```
